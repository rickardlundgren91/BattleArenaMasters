## Target audience and content

Play Console: **Policy and programs > App content > Target audience and content > Start**.

- **Target age groups:** tick **13-15, 16-17 and 18 and over**. Leave 12 and under unticked.
- **Appeal to children:** if asked whether the store listing could unintentionally appeal to children, answer **No**
  (the texts and images do not target children). If Google asks anyway, it may ask for more details; answer honestly.
- **Ads:** No.

### Why 13+ (recommendation for Rickard)
Choosing any age group under 13 puts the game under Google's **Families policy**: only Families-certified SDKs are
allowed (Unity Analytics is not on that list, so analytics would have to go), stricter review, and child-specific
rules for the privacy policy. BAM is a competitive arena game built for teens and adults, its listing is not aimed at
children, and the content rating stays low anyway, so children can still find and play it. Google says not to pick an
older audience just to avoid the Families rules if the game really is made for children; that is not the case here.
