# Privacy Policy – Battle Arena Masters

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Battle Arena Masters ("BAM", "the game") is a mobile game made by **{{DEVELOPER_NAME}}** ("we", "us").
Questions about this policy or your data: **support@battlearenamasters.com**.

## 1. The short version

- BAM is an offline game. You play against computer-controlled bots. There are no accounts, no chat, no ads and no
  in-app purchases.
- Your settings are stored only on your phone.
- The only data that ever leaves your phone is **optional usage data** (it never includes your name or contact details), and only if you tap **"Yes, share"**
  when the game asks, or turn on **Settings > Share usage data**. If you say no, the game sends nothing.
- You can turn it off at any time. Turning it off stops sharing at once and asks our analytics provider to delete what
  was already sent.

## 2. Data stored only on your device

The game saves your settings on your phone: sound volume, graphics quality, left-handed controls, your answer to the
usage data question, whether you finished the tutorial, and the hero and difficulty you picked last. This data is never
sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

## 3. Optional usage data (only with your consent)

If you allow it, the game uses **Unity Analytics**, a service from Unity Technologies, to send the following:

**Game events we record**
- Match started: the hero you picked, the difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard) and whether it was the tutorial match.
- Match ended: the same, plus whether you won or left early, how long the match lasted, your kills, deaths and hero level.
- Tutorial progress: which tutorial step you reached.

**Data Unity Analytics adds automatically**
- A random installation ID created by the Unity software in the game. It is not your name, email, phone number or
  Google account, and it is not the Android advertising ID. A new one is created if you reinstall the game.
- When the game starts and stops, how long you played, and whether it is your first time playing.
- Technical information about your device and the game: device model, Android version, language and region settings,
  screen size, game version and similar app performance information.
- Your IP address, which Unity uses to estimate your approximate location (country and region). Your precise location
  is never collected.

The installation ID links these events together, so under the GDPR this counts as personal data even though it
does not tell us who you are. We do **not** collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, messages or payment information,
and we do not use the advertising ID.

## 4. Why we use it

Only to understand how the game is played and to make it better: for example, to balance the heroes and difficulty
levels, to see where new players get stuck in the tutorial, and to find technical problems on certain devices.
We do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles of you, and we do not sell it.

**Legal basis (EU/EEA and UK):** your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time.

## 5. Your choices and rights

- **Turn it off:** Settings > Share usage data. Collection stops at once, and the game sends Unity a request
  to delete the usage data linked to your installation ID.
- **Ask us:** email support@battlearenamasters.com to ask about, get a copy of, or delete your data. We do not know who you are,
  so the quickest way to delete your data is the Settings switch above; we will help you by email if that is not possible.
- **Uninstalling** the game removes everything stored on your phone and stops all sharing.
- If you are in the EU/EEA you can complain to your data protection authority. In Sweden this is
  Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), www.imy.se.

## 6. Who receives the data

Usage data is processed by **Unity Technologies** as our service provider (data processor), under Unity's terms and
privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. Unity may process the data in countries outside the EU/EEA,
including the United States, using legal safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
We do not share or sell the data to anyone else.

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep usage data only as long as we need it to improve the game, and Unity stores it according to its own data
retention rules. When you turn sharing off or ask us to delete it, a deletion request is sent to Unity.

## 8. Security

Usage data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

## 9. Children

BAM is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you
believe a child has shared usage data, email us and we will have it deleted.

## 10. Changes

If we change what the game collects, we will update this policy and the date above before the change reaches the game,
and we will ask for your consent again if needed.
