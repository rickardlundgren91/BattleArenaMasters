How it plays

Easy to learn. Hard to master.

You won't spend the match tapping on targets. Your hero attacks on its own. Your job is the part that matters: where you stand, which fights you take, and when you fire your special.

1

Pick your hero

Ten heroes, each with a clear job. Hook enemies, shake the ground, shield friends or line up the perfect shot.

Tank · Fighter · Marksman · Assassin · Mage · Siege · Support
2

Move and position

Your hero attacks a random enemy in range. Stand close to the one you want to hit, and stay away from the ones you don't.

One thumb on the joystick
3

Time your special

Tap and it picks a target for you, or drag to aim it yourself. The right special at the right moment wins the fight.

Tap to auto-target · Drag to aim

Learn it in the tutorial

Focus

Focus is how many enemies are in your range. Your hero attacks one of them at random, so fewer targets means more hits where you want them.

Step close to the enemy you want to hit and away from the rest. Make one enemy the only target in your range, and every attack goes to them.

FAQ

Questions

What is Battle Arena Masters?

A fast 3v3 arena game for mobile. Two teams of three heroes fight to destroy each other's tower.

How long is a match?

About 4 minutes on average, usually between 2 and 5. A match ends when one team destroys the other team's tower.

How long does it take to learn?

You'll know the basics after a match or two: move, tap your special, or drag it to aim. Mastering all ten heroes takes a lot longer.

Do I have to aim every attack?

No. Your hero attacks a random enemy in range on its own. You decide where to stand and when to use your special.

When can I play?

The Android playtest opens soon. The test link will be posted here and in our Discord first.

Is it on iPhone?

Not yet. We're starting with Android. Join the Discord to hear when that changes.

Which phones does it run on?

Android first. We'll share more about other platforms later.

What do testers need to do?

Stay in the test for at least two weeks, play when you want, and tell us what you think on Discord.

Is it free?

Yes, the playtest is free.

Can I play with friends?

Yes, team up with friends who are also in the playtest, or play against bots.

Do you collect data?

Only if you say yes. The game asks once whether you want to share usage data, like which heroes you pick and how matches end, to help us balance the game. It never includes your name. You can turn it off in Settings at any time, and we then ask Unity to delete what was sent. Read the Privacy Policy.

How do I report a bug?

Join our Discord and post in #bug-reports. A screenshot and your phone model help a lot.
