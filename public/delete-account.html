Delete your account

Battle Arena Masters · BAM Games (Rickard Lundgren, Sweden)

You only have a Battle Arena Masters account if you signed in with Google Play Games in the game. Playing against bots without signing in creates no account.

In the game (fastest)

  • Open Battle Arena Masters and go to Settings > Account.
  • Tap Delete account and data, then tap again to confirm.

Your account is deleted at once.

Without the game

Email support@battlearenamasters.com with the subject "Delete my BAM account" and your player name. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your player name (it becomes free for others to take).
  • Your level, XP, Rating, match history and stats, and your place on the Top 100.
  • Your friends, friend requests and blocked players.
  • Your Unity player account and its link to your Google Play Games account.

What is kept

  • Reports you sent about other players may be kept for up to 12 months so that repeated abuse can be handled. Then they are deleted.
  • Optional usage data is not linked to your account. To have it deleted, turn off Settings > Share usage data in the game, or email us.
  • Your Google account is not touched.
