Privacy Policy

Battle Arena Masters · Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Applies to the Android playtest and battlearenamasters.com

Battle Arena Masters is in a closed playtest. This page explains in plain words what the test version of the game and this website send, and what you can switch off. It will be updated before the game is released.

Who we are

Battle Arena Masters is made by a small indie team in Sweden. Questions or requests about your data: support@battlearenamasters.com.

This website

  • The site sets no cookies and has no login, forms or ads.
  • It is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and protect it from attacks.
  • If we turn on Cloudflare Web Analytics, it counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across other sites.
  • Links to Discord and Google Play take you to those services, where their own privacy rules apply.

The game: what is sent and when

Your account. The game signs you in to Unity Gaming Services (run by Unity Technologies) with an anonymous player ID. This is how your profile, progress and items are saved in the cloud. You don't need to give an email address to play.

Backup code. If you tap to make a backup code, Unity creates a username and password for your account, and the game copies them to your clipboard as one code so you can restore your progress on another phone. The code is a password: keep it to yourself and never send it to anyone, including us.

Match data. To balance the heroes, finished matches are uploaded to our cloud storage at Unity. This is on by default and is separate from the usage data question below. You choose how much under Settings → Match data → Auto upload:

  • Full match (default): the recording of the match, such as hero, map, result, kills, items, positions and events, the player names in that match, the build number, your phone model, frame rate and any game errors.
  • Results only: hero, map, result, kills, deaths, items and match length (about 1 KB), with no positions, names or error details.
  • Off: matches stay on your phone.

You can also set a daily limit and upload only on Wi-Fi. Matches are deleted from your phone once they are sent.

Usage data (only if you say yes). The first time you reach the home screen, the game asks "Share usage data?". Nothing is sent until you tap Allow. If you do, Unity Analytics receives a few anonymous events: game starts and returning days, tutorial steps, and when a match starts, ends or is left early (mode, hero, map, win or loss, length). No names are sent. Change it any time in Settings → Share usage data.

Playing online and with friends. When you use these features, Unity services handle what they need to work: your player name and profile, rating and leaderboard place (visible to other players), friends and invites, and the connection data for online matches (Unity Lobby and Relay).

Voice chat. If voice chat is in your build, it uses Unity Vivox. Android asks for microphone permission first, and your microphone starts muted.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the game may store a push token so it can tell you about invites and updates.

Reports and settings. If you report a player, the report (player ID, reason and time) is stored so we can look at it. The game may also download settings from Unity Remote Config, and test builds installed outside Google Play check a small version file on Google Drive for updates.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • The playtest has no ads and no ad tracking.
  • We don't ask for your real name, address or payment details.

Who gets it

  • The development team uses match data and usage data to find bugs and balance the game.
  • Unity Technologies stores and processes the game's data for us as part of Unity Gaming Services (accounts, cloud saves, match data, analytics, online play, friends and voice).
  • Cloudflare hosts this website.
  • Google delivers the playtest through Google Play, and hosts the update file for builds installed outside Google Play.
  • Other players can see your player name, rating and leaderboard place.

We don't share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How your data is protected

  • The game talks to Unity Gaming Services over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and this website is only served over HTTPS.
  • Your game data is linked to an anonymous player ID, not to your real name or email address.
  • Only the development team can reach the game's data in Unity's dashboard.
  • Match files are deleted from your phone once they have been uploaded.

No system is completely secure, but we keep what we collect small and limit who can reach it.

How long we keep it

We keep playtest data, such as accounts, progress, match data and usage data, for as long as the playtest runs and for at most 12 months after it ends. After that it is deleted.

Your choices

  • Turn usage data and match upload off in the game's Settings.
  • Leave the playtest from the Google Play test link.
  • Email support@battlearenamasters.com to ask what we have about you or to have it deleted.

How to delete your data

Email support@battlearenamasters.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include your player name, and your Player ID if the game shows it. Don't send your password or backup code. We may ask a question to make sure the account is yours.

We handle deletion requests within 30 days and remove your account, progress, leaderboard place, friends list and the match data linked to you. We'll email you when it's done.

Changes

When the game changes, this page changes too. The date at the top shows the latest version.
